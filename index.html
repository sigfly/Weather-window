How snow is calculated: yr.no provides total precipitation as water equivalent, not snow depth. This app estimates the snow fraction using forecast air temperature (all snow at ≤ 0°C, all rain at ≥ 2°C, linear mix between), then converts snow water equivalent to fresh snow using an approximate 10:1 snow-to-water ratio (1 mm water ≈ 1 cm snow). Actual snow depth depends on snow density, wind, ground conditions and melting. These are estimates, not official snowfall forecasts.
